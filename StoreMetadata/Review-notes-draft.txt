INTERNAL REVIEW DEMO - NOT SUBMITTED

1. No account is required. First launch has two screens: select publisher cards, then preview and Start ticker. Topics/search/browse-language filter discovery without dropping choices. Six suggestions are initially visible, More sources expands the catalog, and Choose for me explicitly selects suggestions. Telegram/RSS is optional. Name/four looks are on the ready screen; detailed appearance, logos and markets are optional. Existing profiles remain unchanged until confirmation; Build my feed reopens the chooser. Space pauses; arrows browse; Esc ends output.
2. The simple setup offers four looks: News, Night, Minimal and Glass. Try a custom station name, PNG/JPEG logo, clock/date, placement and a 50-180 pt strip. Choose one to four rows; each selects Content, My Text or Market prices independently. Market rows share the selected instrument watchlist; the shared speed slider gives each lower row a slightly faster default tempo. Pause affects all rows. Three/four rows raise the minimum total strip height to 84/112 pt. Message separators can be customized.
3. The toolbar contains only Settings. Settings > Saved settings offers Save current settings, named entries with Load, and individual deletion with confirmation. Empty and duplicate names are rejected. Older sets remain readable. Announcements and Phone Remote remain under Display commands.
4. Content contains 18 choices in four groups. Thoughts for Today, Just for Laughs and other original offline collections provide a demonstration without a network.
5. Choose Your Feed opens source details. Pick source language, optional Russian translation, topic/exclusion keywords, optional short headlines, publisher toggles and preview. Selected sources show load/eligibility status. Search sources and visible headlines locally. Starred highlights may extend freshness to less than 48 hours. Other news expires at 24 hours; unknown/future dates are excluded.
6. Russian translation uses Apple Translation on macOS 15+, after system preparation/download consent. The final Mac audit exercised native translation with a synthetic English headline and verified original text/date preservation; prepared system models remain required.
7. Markets uses Coinbase WebSocket, MOEX polling and official CBR/ECB reference rates. Delayed and daily-reference values are labelled. Live network results and provider permissions are separate questions.
8. Phone Remote is off by default. Enable only on a trusted network; use the displayed random local link. It is plain HTTP and is not a cloud service. A full real-phone acceptance test remains pending.
9. Settings offers English/Russian UI, local privacy information and Delete Local Data confirmation. The menu-bar item/Dock reopens the control window.
