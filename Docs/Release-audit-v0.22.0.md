550 unique URLs and identities; 69 public Telegram previews. Multiple topic feeds from a publisher are labelled separately. Freshness in the app remains based on original publication date (24 hours, explicitly selected highlights at most 48). A catalog source may have no currently eligible headline; it is never filled with old posts to make a queue look busy. Offline collections and market instruments are outside these news-source counts.

Discovery used public feed directories, followed by direct HTTPS fetches from each selected publisher/public Telegram preview. The selection rejected failed requests, malformed/non-feed bodies, unsuitable languages, podcasts, unrelated category candidates, exact duplicate feed bodies and sources with no original publication within the last 90 days. That source activity threshold is a curation check, not the application’s item age limit.

Discovery origins: [Plenary list](https://github.com/plenaryapp/awesome-rss-feeds), [specialist feed list](https://github.com/xiangyugongzuoliu/awesome-rss-feeds-list), [NASA published feeds](https://www.nasa.gov/rss-feeds/), official publisher/city-organisation pages. Each shipped URL, observed original-publication timestamp and discovery origin is in the adjacent JSON audit. No fetched article bodies, user preferences or credentials are packaged.

The catalog does not certify editorial accuracy, official account ownership or redistribution rights. Public distribution/App Store and existing provider-term gates remain open.
