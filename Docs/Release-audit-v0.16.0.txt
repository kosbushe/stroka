STROKA 0.16.0 (18) - LOCAL PRODUCT AUDIT
14 November 2026. Product verdict: a functional native beta with a clean source-publication candidate; public distribution and App Store gates remain open.

FIXED NOW
Four concurrent feed requests, a 5 MB received-body cap, HTTPS-only redirects, cancellation of obsolete collection/language requests, immediate refresh after source-language changes, forty-item parser limit and 8192 UTF-16-unit field limit. Oversized fields cannot inflate headline caches. Atom updated alone is excluded as a publication timestamp; original published/pubDate/Dublin Core dates remain supported. Selected sources sort first and show errors, eligible count, translation wait or filter/freshness explanations. Preview headline search is local. Two broken endpoints were replaced with official Moscow news/economy feeds; personal sources were preserved.

CONTENT SNAPSHOT
79/79 current URLs downloaded and parsed: 77 RSS catalog entries plus two public Telegram previews. 64/79 had news eligible under the 24-hour rule at the final native audit: 1093 raw source headlines, 681 at most eleven words. These are up to forty entries per source, not a unique editorial ranking or the entirety of the web. Freshness naturally changes over time. @aiffring had no eligible recent post; @exploitex did. Apple Newsroom entries in this response were updated-only and are not treated as fresh original publications. Source availability, translation rights and public-display permissions are separate issues.
Live market check (28 seconds, default watchlist): MOEX 6, CBR 3, ECB 2, Coinbase 2 values. Provider dates/cadence are retained; not all values are real time.

RENDERER PERFORMANCE
Mac: macOS 26.5.1, Apple Silicon, 1710x1107 logical points, 2x backing, 60 Hz. One-second warmup, six-second window, synthetic market updates every 0.4 seconds, no live network and no concurrent compiler. Small 52 pt dual strip: final repeated candidate runs 3.53% and 3.74% of one CPU core; prior 0.15.1 runs 3.47% and 3.48%. Initial extra runs varied more. There is no demonstrated renderer CPU improvement, and no renderer rewrite was made. RSS work is now bounded independently of animation.
144 pt dual strip: 1.77%; paused case: 0.73%, zero draw calls; hidden case: 0.43%, zero draw calls/callbacks. Scenarios differ; this is not a monotonic size comparison. Visible test callback p95 was 16.67 ms with no intervals over 1.5 frame periods. This measures callback pacing, not compositor-presented frames or hardware-wide smoothness.
Installed 0.16.0 with its control window visible and no visible live strip: 0.429-0.556% CPU, 114.25-114.98 MiB resident, 47.49-48.58 MiB physical footprint in six five-second samples. The prior process had different cache/warmup state; do not claim controlled memory reduction from these samples.

VALIDATION
316 named assertions: 113 core, 20 feed reliability, 16 short content, 18 content catalog, 19 editorial, 25 edge/style, 56 compact layout, 13 dual row, 19 separators, 9 motion and 8 double click. Preview visibility integration also passed. Edge checks cover 288 geometry contexts and 44 pixel renders; dual checks render 36 layouts. Native English/Russian and light/dark UI snapshots were produced. No full physical-input, VoiceOver, long-session, older-OS or external-monitor acceptance is implied.
Technical source preflight, plist/project syntax, Universal architectures, release signature/sandbox/manifest and exclusion of diagnostic entry points passed. A fresh installed 0.16.0 process was verified; saved settings, presets and both language selections compared equal across installation.
The old process did not exit through the external termination request within five seconds; installation used a forced restart after preference backups. Actual user Quit needs explicit acceptance before public release. Offscreen dark toolbar screenshots are not a reliable material/icon acceptance surface; inspect the real native toolbar before store screenshot approval.
